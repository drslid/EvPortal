# What's new in EvPortal

[Open EvPortal](https://drslid.github.io/EvPortal/) · [User guide](https://drslid.github.io/EvPortal/en/aide.html)

## [2.0.0](https://github.com/drslid/EvPortal/releases/tag/v2.0.0) — 2026-09-12

- Transfer shortcut backups between your phone and car with a QR code in either direction, without typing a backup code. Receive a copy with **Add**, then apply it with **Restore**.
- Keep several backups, restore the one you choose and undo the last restoration. Code, link and JSON file backups remain available.
- Organize shortcuts with drag and drop, favorites and personal categories. **All** sorts services by how often you open them.
- Add or remove services from one shared catalogue, or create shortcuts of your own.
- Choose standard or smaller shortcuts, with or without their names. Dark mode is the default; light mode remains one tap away.
- Use EvPortal in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Russian, Arabic or Portuguese, with guides in all eight languages.
- Follow the updated phone-transfer walkthrough and see real examples of the standard and compact interfaces.
